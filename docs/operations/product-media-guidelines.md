# Product media guidelines

Product imagery must accurately represent the item being sold and must be owned by Dylan's Palace, licensed for commercial use, or explicitly supplied by the product owner. Do not populate the live catalogue with unrelated stock images merely to make galleries look fuller.

## Required views

Aim for three to five ordered images per product:

1. primary front or three-quarter view;
2. back or alternate side;
3. material, stitching or hardware detail;
4. styled or on-body view where available;
5. scale, packaging or interior view for bags.

The first uploaded image is the primary catalogue image. The storefront uses a second image for desktop hover when one is available and exposes the full ordered set on the product page.

## Technical guidance

- Use a consistent portrait ratio, preferably 4:5.
- Minimum recommended size: 1200 × 1500 pixels.
- Avoid screenshots, watermarks and heavy text overlays.
- Keep colour, crop and lighting consistent across views.
- Describe the actual view in alt text; the admin currently generates a safe product/view fallback.

## Adding images

Open **Admin → Products**, edit the product, and upload multiple files before saving. Uploads are persisted to UploadThing and ordered in the sequence shown. Product and media database changes are saved transactionally after upload completion.
